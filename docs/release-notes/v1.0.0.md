# DealFlow360 v1.0.0 — Deal Governance Engine

DealFlow360 governs whether a commercial deal is safe to move forward — from quote creation through approval, negotiation, fulfillment, billing, payment, and audit.

### Highlights

- **Multi-Tier Discount Governance**: Tier-based discount ceilings (`BRONZE`, `SILVER`, `GOLD`, `PLATINUM`, `ENTERPRISE`) and category limits.
- **Risk & Margin Evaluation**: Dynamic Risk Score (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`) and Margin Realization Index (MRI).
- **Approval State Machine**: Automatic flagging of commercial policy violations for `SALES_MANAGER` and `FINANCE_MANAGER` review.
- **Customer Counter-Offer Portal**: Isolated portal for customer price negotiation with instant server-side re-evaluation.
- **Multi-Warehouse Fulfillment**: Stock allocation across hubs, backorder tracking, and concurrency-safe stock reservation locks.
- **Hybrid Recurring Billing**: Separate one-time fees from recurring subscriptions (`MONTHLY`, `QUARTERLY`, `YEARLY`).
- **Payment & Credit Workflows**: Payment recording (`ISSUED` $\rightarrow$ `PAID`), subscription cancellation (`ACTIVE` $\rightarrow$ `CANCELLED`), and transactional credit notes (`CN-XXXXXX`).
- **Operational Reporting & Exports**: Canonical dataset filtering with genuine binary `.xlsx` downloads and print-ready PDF export.
- **Security & RBAC**: Firebase Authentication, server-side RBAC, customer tenant isolation, integer minor-unit money, and persistent audit trail.
